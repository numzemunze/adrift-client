Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

ADRIFT ("the Game", "we", "us") is a browser-based sandbox game. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google:

  • Your Google account ID
  • Your email address
  • Your display name and profile picture URL (if provided)

While you play, we store the following on our servers:

  • Your in-game progress: placed blocks, Ether balance, flag state, raids, and other gameplay actions
  • Timestamps of your actions and the time you were last active
  • Your chosen in-game username, bio, avatar, and region
  • Your in-game world coordinates — the position of your island on the game's world map

If you enable push notifications, we also store your device's push endpoint and the encryption keys required to deliver notifications to that device. You can disable notifications at any time in your profile; doing so removes this data from our servers.

We store an authentication token and your display preferences (sound setting, block-shape preset) in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device except as part of the authentication flow.

We do not use your IP address for tracking, we do not use analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not sell any data to third parties. Our hosting provider (Render) logs incoming requests, including IP addresses, for operational purposes such as abuse prevention and debugging. These logs are retained by the provider for a limited time and are not shared with advertisers.

2. How we use it

  • To create and maintain your game account
  • To display your profile to other players (username, bio, avatar, region)
  • To show your island on the world map alongside other players' islands
  • To synchronize game state between your device and our servers
  • To send push notifications that you have enabled (raid alerts, Ether accumulation, long-absence reminders)
  • To prevent abuse, cheating, and multi-accounting

3. Where data is stored

Game data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Game servers run on Render. Authentication is provided by Supabase via Google OAuth. Push notifications are delivered via the browser push service of your platform (Google FCM on Android, Apple Push Notification service on iOS, Mozilla on Firefox). Each of these providers has its own privacy policy and security practices.

4. Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • With Supabase, Render, and platform push services as described above, to operate the Game
  • When required by law

Your in-game username, bio, avatar, public game statistics, and your island's position on the world map are visible to other players. The world map shows in-game coordinates only — we do not collect, request, or infer your real-world location, IP-based geolocation, or GPS data.

5. Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated game data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will remove your data within 30 days.

You can also revoke ADRIFT's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings.

6. Children

The Game is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use ADRIFT.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
